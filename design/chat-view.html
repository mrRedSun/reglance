The user wants breeds that don't bark. The Basenji is the classic answer; mention why (larynx shape) and a couple of quiet relatives.

The Basenji is the famous one. Its larynx is shaped differently, so instead of barking it makes a yodel-like sound called a "barroo."

A few others rarely or never bark:

  • New Guinea Singing Dog – howls in a melodic, almost bird-like way
  • Shiba Inu – quiet, but has the infamous "Shiba scream"
